Hey! I am Archana Vaidheeswaran

As a Program Director at Algoverse, I oversee AI Research programs and help grow global AI safety initiatives through fellowships and community-building. I previously served on the Board of Directors at Women in Machine Learning.

I am the Research Program Director at PRISM (Peer-vetted Research Initiative for Safety Methodologies), a 16-week AI safety research program run under Women Who Do Data and funded by the Long-Term Future Fund (LTFF). PRISM uses peer selection to scale mentorship and turn the senior-researcher bottleneck into a multiplier.

When I'm not thinking about AI, you'll probably find me cheering on indie game creators as a Community Director at the International Game Developers Association (IGDA).
